## Method

1. `zoo replay --raw` into `spanweave-live serve`: every status, every event, the exit status.
2. `zoo replay --json` into `serve`. Completion is `serve --requests N`: after the Nth request the input has ended, and the end of input runs spanweave-live's `Run.finish()`, which replaces the completion with `Cap(0)` and ticks once, so every trace still held completes and is written (its SPEC §8.5 at the pinned sha). That is the `Cap(0)`-at-end the plan names, chosen by the receiver's CLI and not by the audit. Step 2 then runs twice more, each `serve` given one completion policy as well as `--requests N` and each replay `--timing`, the captured gaps slept: `--quiet 0`, then `--root-grace 0`. **The zeros are the audit's values**: the pinned receiver documents no default for either flag (its README, "The CLI"), so the audit chooses the smallest legal value, at which each policy fires at the first tick its condition holds (its SPEC §5.3); a trace neither completed by the Nth request completes at end of input by `Cap(0)`, and each `completed` event's reason says which. Step 3 compares the end-of-input run's graphs; the two policy runs are an observation and yield no finding.
3. Each `json/` body re-read in-process by the pinned `spanweave_live.Endpoint`, each record partitioned by the pinned `trace_id_of`; per partition, the live file against `spanweave.build` of its records as JSON lines (`meta.source_digest` removed, spanweave SPEC §10.4) and against a `spanweave.Builder` fed them in order.
4. `spanweave inspect` on each written graph, verbatim; diagnostics by code, `unmapped_attributes` keys by size, `unknown` nodes; one row per node -- kind, status, model, whether its parent is present, the diagnostics naming it, and its span events as the record carries them.
5. `agentgolden.signature.signature(graph)`, no rules.

Steps 4 and 5 read the `Builder`'s graph (spanweave has no public reader from a graph document back to a `Graph`); step 3 compares its bytes with the live file's. The `listening` port and the clock readings in a `completed` or `late_arrival` event (`seconds`, and the two in its `detail`) are the receiver's and differ run to run; nothing else in this report does.
